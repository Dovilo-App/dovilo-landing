Dovilo docs: the MCP servers and webhooks your agents use.

Two references, one URL and a key.

In short

Dovilo has two MCP servers. Agents MCP at dovilo.app/mcp/agents is for coding agents: 9 tools, no create, no delete, one key per agent. Personal MCP at dovilo.app/mcp is for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok: 13 tools, sign in with your Dovilo account, every write approved by you. Webhooks tell your systems when an AI task moves.
