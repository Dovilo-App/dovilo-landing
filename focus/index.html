Dovilo: focus sessions that fill the whole screen.

A single session, a Pomodoro cycle or a timer on one task. Free and unlimited, no ads.

Get Dovilo See pricing

In short

Dovilo is a free to-do list for iOS and Android with a focus timer that fills the screen. Focus sessions and Pomodoro cycles are free and unlimited, with no ads. The Focus Ambience Pack adds 5 scenes and 16 sound layers for $4.99 once, and it is included with Pro. Finished sessions pay bricks for your city.

Three ways to focus

  • A single session. Any length you choose.
  • A Pomodoro cycle. 25 minutes of focus, a 5 minute break, a longer break every 4 rounds. Breaks start on their own, and only finished rounds count.
  • A timer on one task. The session is bound to that task.

From the first minute to the last

  • A calm start. A breathing exercise and a countdown ease you in.
  • Keeps counting. In the background or with the phone locked.
  • Quiet phone. Do Not Disturb can silence other apps for the session.
  • A clear end. A notification when the session is over.

Six scenes, one of them free

Each scene has its own weather, skyline tint and sound, and fills the whole screen.

  • Default. Always free, and silent.
  • Rainy City. Rain and a fireplace.
  • Midnight. City hum and brown noise. It stays dark even in the light theme.
  • Dusk. Birds and a breeze. Breaks switch to Dusk on their own.
  • Snow. Winter wind and pink noise.
  • Fog. A fog horn and white noise.

The five scenes after Default come with the Focus Ambience Pack.

Mix your own sound

Blend up to 3 of 16 layers, each at its own level: 13 recordings such as rain, a café or a train, and brown, pink and white noise. The mix can follow the session: soft in the first 5 minutes, brighter in the last 2.

Finished sessions build a city

A completed session pays bricks based on its length. Bricks buy houses, offices, parks and roads in your city, and nothing decays.

Straight answers

Is Focus free?

Yes. Single sessions and Pomodoro cycles are free and unlimited, with the Default scene. The Focus Ambience Pack adds the other 5 scenes and 16 sound layers for $4.99 once, and Pro includes it.

Does the timer run with the phone locked?

Yes. It keeps counting in the background and with the phone locked, and a notification tells you when the session ends.

Do unfinished sessions count?

A session ended early still saves, but only finished Pomodoro rounds count toward the daily round counter.

Is Focus on Dovilo Desktop?

No. Focus and the city are on iOS and Android. Desktop is the Windows and Mac client for your lists and AI tasks.
