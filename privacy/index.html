Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Your Privacy is Our Priority

Dovilo ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use Dovilo: the mobile and desktop apps, the website dovilo.app, and the AI connections described in Section 6.

1. Data Collection

By default, Dovilo stores all your tasks, city progress, focus session data, and app preferences locally on your device. During this anonymous phase, no personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored on external servers. You can use Dovilo fully offline without ever sharing any information. Personal data leaves your device only when you sign in (Section 2) or connect an AI assistant or coding agent (Section 6). Anonymized usage analytics are described in Section 3.

2. Cloud Sync & Authentication

If you choose to enable cloud sync, we use Firebase Authentication to verify your identity via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In. In this case, the following data is stored securely on Firebase servers:

  • Your unique User ID (generated by Firebase)
  • Your task and city progress data
  • App preferences and settings
  • If you allow notifications, a push token for your device, used only to send the reminders and alerts you turn on.

Firebase Authentication stores the email address your sign-in provider shares with us (with Apple, this can be a private relay address). We never see or store your password. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on Firebase servers. Section 6 describes the data we process when you connect an AI assistant or coding agent.

3. Analytics

To improve Dovilo and understand how features are used, we use Firebase Analytics. This service collects aggregated, anonymized usage data such as feature engagement, session duration, and app performance metrics. This data does not identify you personally and is used solely for product improvement.

4. Subscription Management

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat processes transaction data provided by Apple App Store and Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment details (credit card numbers, billing addresses, etc.). RevenueCat's privacy practices comply with GDPR and CCPA standards.

5. Third-Party Services

Dovilo integrates with the following third-party services, each compliant with global privacy standards:

  • Firebase and Google Cloud (Google) for authentication, database, analytics, server functions and encryption keys.
  • RevenueCat, for subscription and purchase management.
  • Apple and Google, as sign-in providers and app distributors.
  • Cloudflare, which routes and protects requests to dovilo.app, including AI connections.

We don't sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with the services listed above, and with the AI assistants, coding agents and webhook URLs you connect yourself (Section 6).

6. AI Connections

Dovilo can connect to AI assistants and coding agents. Every connection is optional, starts only when you set it up, and can be removed at any time.

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok). You connect an assistant by signing in with your Dovilo account and approving a consent screen.

  • The assistant can send Dovilo plans, tasks, changes to your tasks and proposals for AI tasks. These wait in the Dovilo app until you approve them.
  • Dovilo sends the assistant only what you allow for that connection: the names of your visible lists; the tasks in those lists if read access is on; and, if AI tasks access is on, the briefs and AI tasks of your visible projects, including their steps, agent notes and your replies.
  • The assistant's provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI) receives this data as part of your conversation and handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • You can change what a connection can see, or remove it, in the Dovilo app under Connected AI apps. Removing a connection stops its access immediately.
  • If you connect an assistant before you have used the Dovilo app, we create an account for that sign-in so your plan can wait for you in the app. Accounts that are never used in the app may be deleted after 30 days.

Coding agents (Pro). Coding agents such as Claude Code or Cursor work on AI tasks through Dovilo's MCP server at dovilo.app/mcp/agents, with an API key you create for each agent in the app.

  • We store only a hash of each key. The key is shown to you once.
  • An agent sees only the names, briefs and AI tasks of the projects you assign to it. It never sees your personal tasks in My Tasks. It can read and update the status, steps, notes and telemetry of those AI tasks and cancel a task with a reason. It cannot create or delete tasks.
  • Anything your agent sends to its own model provider is governed by that provider's privacy policy, not ours.
  • Desktop versions before 2.0 run the MCP server locally on your computer; its traffic does not reach our servers.

Webhooks. If you add a webhook URL to a project, we send events about its AI tasks, including the task's details, to that URL. Desktop delivers events to local addresses itself. Secrets for webhooks we deliver are stored encrypted.

Security records. For AI connections and agent requests we process IP addresses and request details to protect the service and apply rate limits. Rate-limit records are kept for up to 2 days and audit logs for up to 90 days. Access tokens for AI connections expire after 1 hour and refresh tokens after 90 days; we store them only in hashed form.

7. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete the app. Cloud-synced data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

  • Plans and changes proposed by an AI assistant that you don't act on expire after 14 days.
  • AI connections are kept until you remove them or delete your account. Security records are kept as described in Section 6.
  • Deleting your account also deletes your AI connections, agent keys, AI proposals, webhook secrets and security records.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time through the app
  • Request a copy of your data by email — an in-app export is on the way, and will be announced in the changelog
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling it in app settings
  • Remove an AI connection in Connected AI apps, and revoke or replace an agent's key in AI Tasks.

If you are located in the EU, you also have rights under GDPR including the right to rectification, restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Dovilo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page. Continued use of Dovilo after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support [at] dovilo.app.
