In short

Dovilo is a to-do app with a separate task list for AI coding agents. Agents connect to dovilo.app/mcp/agents over MCP: 9 tools, no create, no delete, and your personal list is never visible. ChatGPT or Claude can plan the work; every instruction waits for your approval. Pro, $2.49 a month, 15 AI tasks a month.

How it works

  1. 01 Write the task Or let ChatGPT or Claude propose it.
  2. 02 Approve it on your phone Nothing reaches an agent before you say yes.
  3. 03 Your agent works it Steps and notes land on the task as it runs.
  4. 04 It asks, you answer It stops at a decision; you reply from your phone or chat.

Plain MCP, no lock-in

  • ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok plan the work and send it to you for approval.
  • Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Windsurf and Claude Desktop work the tasks.
  • LangGraph, CI jobs and any MCP client connect with the same URL and key.

Setup is a URL and a key

Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http --scope local dovilo https://dovilo.app/mcp/agents --header "Authorization: Bearer <API_KEY>"

Dovilo Desktop writes the config for 7 clients. Then write a project brief and an AI task.

What the AI can and cannot do

  • Its own tasks, nothing else.
  • The AI proposes. You approve.
  • Your coding work stays out of your chat app unless you turn it on.
  • No delete tool. Not now, not later.
  • Revoke any connection from your phone, instantly.

Straight answers

Does my agent need Dovilo Desktop running?

No. Agents connect to dovilo.app/mcp/agents directly. Desktop is the Windows and Mac client for AI tasks, keys, briefs and webhooks.

What does the agent see?

The AI tasks of the projects it is assigned to, each project's brief, and your replies. Your personal to-do list is not on the tool surface at all.

Can an agent delete a task?

No. There is no delete tool on either server. An agent can cancel a task with a reason, and the task stays visible.

Can I run it in CI or on a server?

Yes. The same URL and key work from any machine. One key per agent, revocable from your phone.

I used Desktop 1.8. What changed?

1.8's local server is gone. Create an agent in the app, copy its key, and point your client at the remote URL. Steps in the MCP docs.
