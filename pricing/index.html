Dovilo pricing: free to use, $2.49 a month for Pro.

The personal app is free and unlimited. Pro adds the AI layer, Desktop, sync and both packs.

In short

Dovilo is free on iOS and Android: unlimited tasks, lists and focus sessions, no ads, offline-first. Pro is $2.49 a month, $19.99 a year or $49.99 once, with a 7-day trial started in the app.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are managed in the App Store or Google Play; Pro stays on until the end of the billing period. Desktop needs Pro, started in the mobile app; there is no desktop-only trial.

Straight answers

Is Dovilo free?

Yes. Dovilo is free to download and use. The free tier includes full to-do management, unlimited tasks and categories, unlimited focus sessions (single and Pomodoro), basic city building, dark & light themes, and zero ads. Dovilo Pro adds planning with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, 15 AI tasks a month for coding agents, the desktop app for Windows and Mac, cloud sync, the Focus Ambience Pack, all buildings and advanced statistics.

How many AI tasks does Pro include?

15 AI tasks a month. A task counts when it opens to your agent, not when you create it, and replies on the same task cost nothing. If you need more, one-time top-up packs add 10, 30 or 75 AI tasks, start at $0.99 and never expire.

Do I need the Focus Ambience Pack?

No. Focus sessions and Pomodoro cycles are free and unlimited, and the Default scene is always available and is simply silent. The pack adds the 5 atmospheric scenes and the 16 sound layers you can blend three at a time. It is a one-time $4.99 purchase, and Pro subscribers already have it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To cancel, go to your device's subscription settings. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
