In short

Dovilo is a free to-do list for iOS and Android with a Pomodoro focus timer and an isometric city builder. Unlimited tasks and focus sessions, no ads, offline-first, 5 languages. Finished work earns bricks, bricks buy buildings, and nothing decays. ChatGPT or Claude can plan your day into it; you approve every plan. Pro is $2.49 a month.

How it works

  1. 01 Plan Write tasks, or let ChatGPT or Claude propose them.
  2. 02 Approve Nothing lands in your list until you tap yes.
  3. 03 Focus A session, a Pomodoro cycle, or a timer on one task.
  4. 04 Build Bricks from finished work buy a city that stays.

Focus that fills the whole screen

A single session, a Pomodoro cycle or a timer on one task, free and unlimited. The Focus Ambience Pack adds 5 scenes and 16 sound layers, 3 at a time: $4.99 once, included with Pro.

A city that stays

Tasks and focus sessions pay bricks. Spend them on houses, offices, parks and roads, placed where you want. Nothing decays, no streak punishes a quiet week. Large builds come with the Full City Package, $4.99 once, included with Pro.

Straight answers

What is Dovilo?

Dovilo is a gamified productivity app that combines a to-do list, focus sessions with full-screen ambience, and an isometric city builder. Complete your tasks and focus sessions to earn bricks, then build houses, offices, parks, and more in your own virtual city.

Is Dovilo free?

Yes. The free tier includes full to-do management, unlimited tasks and categories, unlimited focus sessions, basic city building, dark & light themes, and zero ads. Pro adds planning with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, 15 AI tasks a month for coding agents, the desktop app for Windows and Mac, cloud sync, the Focus Ambience Pack, all buildings and advanced statistics, from $2.49 a month, and it starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can try everything before paying. Cancel before the week is up and nothing is charged.

Can I plan with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok?

Yes. Add Dovilo to the assistant as a custom connector with the URL https://dovilo.app/mcp and sign in with your Dovilo account. The assistant proposes a plan, the plan lands in the AI inbox on your phone, and it goes into your lists with one tap once you approve it. For each connection you choose what the assistant can read and which lists and projects it sees, and its changes always wait for your approval; the setting is called Ask me first. There is no delete tool, and you can revoke a connection from your phone at any time. Sending a plan works on Free; approving it, and everything else, needs Pro.

How does Focus work?

Focus was rebuilt. You can run a single session, a Pomodoro cycle (25 minutes of focus, a 5 minute break, a longer break every 4 rounds), or a session bound to one specific task. The timer keeps counting while the app is in the background or your phone is locked, Do Not Disturb silences other apps during the session, and every scene brings its own ambience: Rainy City, Midnight, Dusk, Snow or Fog.

Do I need the Focus Ambience Pack?

No. Focus sessions and Pomodoro cycles are free and unlimited, and the Default scene is always available and is simply silent. The Focus Ambience Pack adds the 5 atmospheric scenes and 16 sound layers you can blend three at a time. It is a one-time $4.99 purchase, and it is already included with Pro.

Does Dovilo work offline?

Yes. Dovilo is offline-first. All your tasks, focus sessions, and city progress are stored locally on your device. Cloud sync is an optional Pro feature for keeping data across multiple devices.

Does Dovilo have ads?

No. Dovilo is completely ad-free, both in the free tier and in Pro. We believe ads break focus, which is the opposite of what a productivity app should do.

Does my coding agent need Dovilo Desktop running?

No. Since Dovilo 2.0, coding agents such as Claude Code and Cursor connect to Dovilo's remote MCP server at https://dovilo.app/mcp/agents with a key you create in the app, from your laptop, a server or a CI runner. Desktop does not need to be open; it is the desktop client for your lists and AI tasks. The agent sees only the AI tasks of the projects you assign to it, and it cannot create or delete tasks. Agents are part of Pro. Coming from Desktop 1.8? Its built-in MCP server stops working with 2.0, so reconnect your agent with the new URL and a new key. Setup is on the developer page.

How many AI tasks does Pro include?

15 AI tasks a month. A task counts when it opens to your agent, not when you create it, and replies on the same task cost nothing. If you need more, one-time top-up packs add 10, 30 or 75 AI tasks, start at $0.99 and never expire.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To cancel, go to your device's subscription settings. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
