How it works

How it works

One loop, and what it costs

Finish a task or a focus session and Dovilo pays you in bricks. Bricks are the only currency in the app: you spend them in the Market on houses, offices, parks, roads and decorations, then place them on the isometric grid. Nothing decays, no streak punishes you for a quiet week — the city is a record of work you actually did, and it stays.

Tasks and focus sessions are free and unlimited, ads never appear on any tier, and the app is offline-first: your tasks live on the device and work with no connection. Pro ($2.49/month, $19.99/year, or $49.99 once) adds planning with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, where the assistant proposes and you approve in Dovilo; 15 AI tasks a month for coding agents like Claude Code and Cursor; the Windows and Mac desktop app; cloud sync across devices; the Focus Ambience Pack; every building and decoration; and advanced statistics. It starts with a 7-day free trial. Two content packs are one-time $4.99 purchases and are included with Pro, so the parts you want most are never a rented subscription.

What Dovilo does not have yet: a data export. It is on the roadmap, and when it ships it will appear in the changelog along with everything else that changes.

Why it is built this way

A to-do list is a ledger of things you have not done — it is designed to make you feel behind. Dovilo keeps the ledger, but pays the work out into something that accumulates instead of resetting. The city is not a reward scheme bolted onto a task manager; it is the part you look at when you want evidence that the last three months went somewhere.

Features

To-do, focus and city, in detail

Smart Tasks

Due dates, priorities, stars and flags — with one view picker for My Day, Important, Planned, Flagged and every list you keep. Repeats and reminders carry the work that comes back, and lists group themselves into Today, Tomorrow and No date. Every task opens a detail sheet where its properties and your notes sit together.

Focus Sessions

A single session, a Pomodoro cycle, or a session bound to one task. The timer keeps counting in the background and Do Not Disturb holds everything else off.

Ambience

Rainy City, Midnight, Dusk, Snow and Fog fill the whole screen. Blend up to three sounds — rain, fireplace, forest, café — and set the level of each.

City Builder

Tasks and focus sessions both pay out in bricks, and bricks turn into houses, offices, parks and roads that you place yourself on the isometric grid. The city only grows on work you actually finished — nothing appears on its own, and nothing decays while you are away. Demolish a building and part of its cost comes back, so the layout stays yours to redraw.

Reward Market

Spend the bricks you earn on buildings, roads, decorations and upgrades, then place them wherever the grid has room. The Market also carries the one-time content packs and the occasional brick or permit boost. Free opens with a basic set; Pro and the Full City Package unlock every piece.

Track Progress

Completion rate, daily averages, streaks and the monthly trend, next to a city overview with its level, buildings and population. Focus milestones unlock titles, so a long run of sessions reads as more than a number. Advanced statistics come with Pro.

Dark & Light

One design system across every screen — typography, spacing and colour from the same tokens, in both themes.

5 Languages

Available in English, Turkish, Spanish, French and German. Dovilo picks the language up from your device settings, and you can change it by hand in the app at any time.

New — Focus

Focus that fills the whole screen

The Focus tab was rebuilt from the ground up. Pick a session, pick a mood, and the screen becomes the room you work in — rain on the window, a fireplace behind you, and a skyline that lights up brick by brick while the timer runs.

  • Three ways to focus. A single session, a Pomodoro cycle — 25 minutes of focus, a 5 minute break, a longer one every 4 rounds — or a session bound to one specific task.
  • Scenes fill the screen. Rainy City, Midnight, Dusk, Snow and Fog — each with its own weather, its own skyline tint and its own sound. When your break starts, the scene moves to Dusk on its own.
  • Blend up to three sounds. Rain, fireplace, forest, waves, café, library, train, thunder, brown, pink and white noise — each with its own level. The mix follows the session rhythm: it opens softly in the first 5 minutes and brightens in the last 2.
  • It keeps running. Switch apps or lock your phone and the time is still counted correctly. Do Not Disturb silences everything else, and a notification lands the moment the session is done.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Dovilo?

Dovilo is a gamified productivity app that combines a to-do list, focus sessions with full-screen ambience, and an isometric city builder. Complete your tasks and focus sessions to earn bricks, then build houses, offices, parks, and more in your own virtual city.

Is Dovilo free?

Yes! The free tier includes full to-do management, unlimited tasks and categories, unlimited focus sessions, basic city building, dark & light themes, and zero ads. Pro adds planning with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, 15 AI tasks a month for coding agents, the desktop app for Windows and Mac, cloud sync, the Focus Ambience Pack, all buildings and advanced statistics, from $2.49 a month, and it starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can try everything before paying. Cancel before the week is up and nothing is charged.

Can I plan with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok?

Yes. Add Dovilo to the assistant as a custom connector with the URL https://dovilo.app/mcp and sign in with your Dovilo account. The assistant proposes a plan, the plan lands in the AI inbox on your phone, and it goes into your lists with one tap once you approve it. For each connection you choose what the assistant can read and which lists and projects it sees, and by default its changes wait for your approval: the setting is called Ask me first. There is no delete tool, and you can revoke a connection from your phone at any time. Sending a plan works on Free; approving it, and everything else, needs Pro.

What is new in the Focus tab?

Focus was rebuilt. You can run a single session, a Pomodoro cycle (25 minutes of focus, a 5 minute break, a longer break every 4 rounds), or a session bound to one specific task. The timer keeps counting while the app is in the background or your phone is locked, Do Not Disturb silences other apps during the session, and every scene brings its own ambience — Rainy City, Midnight, Dusk, Snow or Fog.

Do I need the Focus Ambience Pack?

No. Focus sessions and Pomodoro cycles are free and unlimited, and the Default scene is always available — it is simply silent. The Focus Ambience Pack adds the 5 atmospheric scenes and 16 sound layers you can blend three at a time. It is a one-time $4.99 purchase, and it is already included with Pro.

Does Dovilo work offline?

Yes! Dovilo is offline-first. All your tasks, focus sessions, and city progress are stored locally on your device. Cloud sync is an optional Pro feature for keeping data across multiple devices.

Does Dovilo have ads?

No. Dovilo is completely ad-free, both in the free tier and in Pro. We believe ads break focus, which is the opposite of what a productivity app should do.

Does my coding agent need Dovilo Desktop running?

No. Since Dovilo 2.0, coding agents such as Claude Code and Cursor connect to Dovilo's remote MCP server at https://dovilo.app/mcp/agents with a key you create in the app, from your laptop, a server or a CI runner. Desktop does not need to be open; it is the desktop client for your lists and AI tasks. The agent sees only the AI tasks of the projects you assign to it, and it cannot create or delete tasks. Agents are part of Pro. Coming from Desktop 1.8? Its built-in MCP server stops working with 2.0, so reconnect your agent with the new URL and a new key. Setup is on the developer page.

How many AI tasks does Pro include?

15 AI tasks a month. A task counts when it opens to your agent, not when you create it, and replies on the same task cost nothing. If you need more, one-time top-up packs add 10, 30 or 75 AI tasks, start at $0.99 and never expire.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To cancel, go to your device's subscription settings. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
