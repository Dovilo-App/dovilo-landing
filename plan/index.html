Dovilo: plan with ChatGPT or Claude, approve in one tap.

Ask your AI for a plan. It lands in Dovilo's inbox, and nothing joins your lists until you say yes.

Get Dovilo Connect your AI app

In short

Dovilo is a free to-do list for iOS and Android. Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok and ask for a plan for your day, an event or a project; it arrives in Dovilo's AI inbox, you change what you like and add it to your lists with one tap. You choose which lists each app can see. Connecting and sending plans is free; adding a plan to your lists is part of Pro, $2.49 a month.

How a plan reaches your list

  1. 01 Ask "Plan my week", "Break this move into tasks": in your AI app, in your words.
  2. 02 It arrives The plan lands in your AI inbox in Dovilo, with a notification.
  3. 03 Change it Edit, drop or move any task before it joins a list.
  4. 04 Approve One tap adds it to your lists. Nothing lands before that.

Connect your AI app

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok connect to Dovilo's Personal MCP at https://dovilo.app/mcp. Sign in to Dovilo first; when your AI app asks you to sign in, use the same Google or Apple account. In the app, Settings > AI > Connected AI apps walks you through the same steps.

ChatGPT
  1. Open chatgpt.com/plugins.
  2. Choose "+", then "Add custom MCP server".
  3. Name it Dovilo and paste the address.
  4. Sign in with your Dovilo account and approve the consent screen.

On the web. Whether you can add a custom MCP server depends on your ChatGPT plan.

Claude
  1. Open Customize > Connectors.
  2. Choose "Add custom connector".
  3. Name it Dovilo and paste the address.
  4. Choose "Connect", sign in with your Dovilo account and approve the consent screen.

On claude.ai or Claude Desktop; it then works in the mobile app too. The free plan allows one custom connector.

Gemini
  1. Open gemini.google.com > Settings > Connected Apps.
  2. Choose "Add a custom app".
  3. Paste the address, then choose "Next".
  4. Sign in with your Dovilo account and approve the consent screen.

On the Gemini web app. Google offers this for personal accounts in the US, age 18 or over, with "Keep Activity" on.

Grok
  1. Open grok.com/connectors.
  2. Choose "New Connector", then "Custom".
  3. Paste the address.
  4. Sign in with your Dovilo account and approve the consent screen.

Then ask it for a plan in a new chat, for example: "Plan my tomorrow in Dovilo with 3 small tasks." The plan appears in your AI inbox; review it and approve it. Connecting and sending plans is free; adding a plan to your lists needs Pro.

Menu names are each app's own English labels, taken from its help page on 20 October 2026: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok. If a menu has moved, the help page has the current one.

What ChatGPT or Claude can see and change

You set this per app in Settings > AI > Connected AI apps, and change it any time.

  • Only the lists you pick. Hidden lists are invisible to it.
  • Read access is yours to turn on or off. With it off, it can still send plans.
  • Every new task or edit waits for you. The setting is called Ask me first.
  • No delete tool. Not now, not later.
  • Your coding work stays out. Unless you turn on AI task access for that app.
  • Disconnect from your phone. Any app, any time.

Straight answers

Which AI apps work with Dovilo?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. Each needs a plan or account that allows a custom connector: ChatGPT depends on your plan, Claude's free plan allows one custom connector, and Gemini offers it to personal accounts in the US aged 18 or over. The steps are under Connect your AI app.

Can it see all my tasks?

No. It sees only the lists you pick for that app, and only if read access is on. You can change both from your phone at any time.

Does planning work on the free tier?

Connecting and sending plans is free. Adding a plan to your lists is part of Pro, with a 7-day trial in the app.

Is Dovilo in the ChatGPT or Claude app directory?

Not yet. You add it by its address, https://dovilo.app/mcp, in a few steps.

Does it work offline?

Your lists do. Plans need a connection: they travel from your AI app to Dovilo's server and on to your phone.
