What a gamified to-do list does once it is installed

Dovilo attaches the game to finishing the work, not to planning it. Every task you complete and every focus session you run pays out in bricks, and bricks are the only currency in the Market, where you buy the houses, offices, parks and roads you then place yourself on an isometric grid. The city is the record of what you actually got through, which is what makes it worth opening on a day the list is not.

Four things live in the one app. Tasks carry due dates, repeats, reminders, stars, flags and your own lists, behind a single view picker holding My Day, Important, Planned, Flagged and every custom list with a live count. Focus runs in three shapes: one session of any length from 1 to 180 minutes, a Pomodoro cycle of 25 minutes with a 5 minute break and a longer break every 4 rounds, or a session bound to one task. The timer keeps counting while the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked. Ambience fills the screen for the length of a session: six scenes, and up to three sound layers blended at once out of sixteen. It is ambience rather than music, so there is no melody or beat to follow. AI tasks are a separate list you keep for coding agents, which work through it over remote MCP from any machine.

All of it is offline-first: tasks, focus history and the city sit on the device, and no internet connection is needed to use the app. Cloud sync is optional and comes with Pro. There are no ads on any tier, the app ships in English, Turkish, Spanish, French and German, and both the dark and the light theme are free.

Which download you need

The mobile apps are the free ones, and free here is the whole product rather than a trial: a download from the App Store or Google Play gives you full to-do management, unlimited single and Pomodoro focus sessions, the silent Default ambience scene, city building, both themes and no ads. The desktop apps are the Pro half. They carry two task surfaces on a full-size screen, full to-do management for your own list and AI task management for your agents, with focus sessions and the city staying on mobile. Desktop is also where you set up the signed webhooks, so it needs an active subscription, which you start in the mobile app. Coding agents do not need it: they connect to Dovilo directly, with a key you can create on the phone or on the desktop.

What is free, and what Pro adds

Pro is $2.49 a month, $19.99 a year or $49.99 once, and it begins with a 7-day free trial you start in the iOS or Android app. It adds planning with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, approved in Dovilo; AI Agents like Claude Code and Cursor working through 15 AI tasks a month over remote MCP; the Windows and Mac desktop app, where webhooks are set up; cloud sync across devices; both one-time packs, the Focus Ambience Pack and the Full City Package; advanced statistics; +6 build permits a day; and a 30-minute snooze from the reminder notification. The same subscription is what unlocks Desktop, so the desktop app is never a separate purchase.

Two one-time packs sit outside the subscription: the Focus Ambience Pack at $4.99, which adds the five atmospheric scenes and the sixteen sound layers, and the Full City Package at $4.99, which adds every building, decoration and upgrade. Neither is a subscription, and Pro already includes both. AI task packs start at $0.99 for 10, 30 or 75 extra agent tasks, and they never expire. Prices are the USD store baseline; each store applies its own regional pricing.

Download questions

Which Dovilo downloads are free?

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are free and ad-free. The free tier covers full to-do management, unlimited single and Pomodoro focus sessions, the silent Default ambience scene, basic city building and both the dark and the light theme. The Windows and Mac desktop apps are the two downloads that need an active Pro subscription.

How do I install Dovilo Desktop on Windows or Mac?

Subscribe to Pro in the iOS or Android app first: the desktop app needs an active subscription, and the same subscription unlocks it, so there is no separate desktop purchase. Then download Dovilo-Setup.exe or Dovilo.dmg from the desktop section of this page. Both come from Dovilo's public releases repository, and the app updates itself from there afterwards.

Can I try Pro before paying for it?

Yes. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial, begun from the iOS or Android app. After that it is $2.49 a month, $19.99 a year or $49.99 once. Prices are the USD store baseline, and each store applies its own regional pricing.

Do I have to download Dovilo again when it updates?

No. On iPhone, iPad and Android, updates arrive through the App Store and Google Play like any other app: automatically if automatic updates are on, otherwise from the Updates list in the store. Dovilo Desktop updates itself and picks up each new build on its own. This page is only for the first install.

Which download do I need to connect an AI coding agent?

None beyond the phone app. Since Dovilo 2.0, a coding agent such as Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex CLI or Gemini CLI connects to Dovilo's server at https://dovilo.app/mcp/agents with a key you create in the iOS or Android app, or in Dovilo Desktop, so the agent can run on any machine and Desktop does not need to be running. Agents reach AI tasks only, the jobs you write for them in the AI Tasks tab, never your personal to-do list, and they cannot create or delete tasks. You need Pro and Dovilo 2.0 or later.
